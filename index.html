How we build

Same rules for every app

Rooted in daily life

Each app starts from something people in India already do every day, then removes the friction from it.

Private by default

Where it can, the work happens on your own device. Freeflo and Kept never need to send your voice anywhere.

Made with restraint

Few screens, few settings, plain words. If a feature needs a tutorial, it gets cut or redesigned.
